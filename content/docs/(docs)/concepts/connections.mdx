title: Connections and secrets
description: Workflows use your app accounts through connections, and your own API keys as secrets. Both are encrypted, used by name, and never shown in a workflow or to your AI.
icon: KeyRound
---

A workflow never holds a password or an API key. It names the account or key it needs, and Lodol supplies it when a step runs. That keeps credentials out of workflows, out of your AI tool's chat, and out of anything you share.

## Connections

A **connection** is your workspace's access to one of your apps, such as your Google account or your Slack workspace. App actions and triggers run through it.

- **You connect an app once.** When a workflow first uses an app your workspace hasn't connected, its step shows a **Connect** button. Some apps ask you to sign in; others ask for an API key from the app's own settings, such as a Stripe key from the Stripe dashboard.
- **A connection is shared or personal.** Whoever connects an app chooses, under **Who can use it**, between everyone in the workspace, specific roles, or only themselves; sharing one needs the **Manage integrations** permission. A shared connection means an app is connected once for everyone. Apps that reach one person's own data, such as Gmail, start out as **Only me**, and some, such as Google Drive, Google Sheets and Dropbox, are always personal, so each person connects their own.
- **AI models use your own key.** The [AI agent](/docs/guides/ai-agents) step runs on your connection to an AI provider such as Anthropic, OpenAI or Google Gemini, so the provider bills your account directly. Connect one under **Integrations** in Lodol.

Your AI tool can't connect apps for you: connecting always happens in Lodol, by someone in your workspace.

## Secrets

A **secret** is a key or password you add yourself, for a service that has no app in Lodol. A [Call an API](/docs/guides/http-and-code#call-an-api) step uses it by name, usually in an `Authorization` header.

- Secrets are encrypted at rest, and credentials are encrypted in transit.
- Workflows and AI tools see only a secret's name, never its value.
- A secret can belong to the whole workspace or to one workflow. Deleting a workflow keeps its secrets: they stay with it while it's in the Trash, and once it's deleted for good they're detached from it, not deleted.

<Callout type="warn" title="For apps Lodol has, use their actions">
  A **Call an API** step sends only the secrets you give it, unless you choose a connection in its **Connection** field, which some apps allow, such as Google's, HubSpot and Notion: then it's signed in as that account, and can only call that app's own servers. Use that for an endpoint the app's actions don't cover, not in place of its actions.
</Callout>

## Values

Values are named settings your workspace keeps that aren't secret, such as a base URL several workflows share. Steps read them as `@values.NAME`. Workflows can also save and update values as they run, to [remember things between runs](/docs/guides/stored-values).
